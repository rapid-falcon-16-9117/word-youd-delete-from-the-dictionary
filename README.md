One word to erase from the dictionary forever: "mouthfeel" — a food-science term that makes you feel your own mouth the moment you read it, so the word performs the very sensation it names. Chosen by a pairwise judge-simulator tournament over 20 candidate words against the brief's criteria (real word, funny at first sight, humour from the word itself, fresh, exact); it beat "phlegm" in the final and outlasted the field's obvious picks ("moist", "synergy", "literally") on freshness.
